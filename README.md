# Instacart Customer Behavior Analysis

# Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using the Instacart Online Grocery Shopping Dataset. The objective is to clean the data, perform exploratory data analysis (EDA), and uncover insights into customer ordering patterns, product popularity, and reorder behavior.

The analysis focuses on understanding when customers place orders, how frequently they shop, which products are most popular, and the factors that contribute to repeat purchases.

# Business Problem
Instacart is an online grocery delivery platform that allows customers to purchase groceries and have them delivered to their homes. Understanding customer purchasing habits can help businesses:

Improve inventory planning
Optimize product recommendations
Increase customer retention
Forecast demand more accurately
Identify high-value products and customers

## Key Findings

# Customer Ordering Patterns
Most orders are placed during late morning and early afternoon hours.
Customers tend to shop on specific days rather than evenly throughout the week.
Strong weekly purchasing patterns were observed.
Order Behavior
Most customers place a relatively small number of orders.
Most orders contain fewer than 15 products.
Large orders are uncommon and represent a small portion of total purchases.

# Product Insights

The most frequently ordered and reordered products include:

Banana
Bag of Organic Bananas
Organic Strawberries
Organic Baby Spinach
Organic Hass Avocado

Fresh produce and organic products dominate customer purchases.

# Reorder Behavior
Many customers repeatedly purchase the same products.
Certain grocery staples show exceptionally high reorder rates.
Customer loyalty is strongest for frequently consumed everyday products.

# Conclusions

The analysis shows that Instacart customers exhibit predictable shopping habits centered around recurring grocery purchases. Fresh produce and organic products are consistently among the most purchased and reordered items, indicating strong customer loyalty toward staple household goods. These insights can support inventory management, customer retention strategies, product recommendations, and demand forecasting initiatives.
